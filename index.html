Settings

Find3r connects for you. No wallet, account, or special setup needed. You can choose your own connection below.

Opening apps

Compatible apps can read blockchain data through Find3r. Your private connection URL stays outside the app. This does not connect your wallet or approve transactions.

Advanced connection settings

Use your own RPC or local node. Connection keys are never passed to apps you open.

Images & files

Images and files stored outside the blockchain need a content gateway. Find3r tries another if one is busy. You can choose your own.

Find more artworks

An artwork index helps find collections across a large wallet. Find3r provides one when available. Without it, familiar collections and direct artwork links still work. An Alchemy RPC URL in your connection settings also enables its NFT index.

Optional: https://eth-mainnet.g.alchemy.com/nft/v3/YOUR_KEY/ (use the matching network). Leave blank to use the default.
